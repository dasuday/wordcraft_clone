## Conventions

- Informal "du" form throughout; imperative for commands: **Gem**, **Indsæt tabel**, **Åbn**, **Fortryd**, **Omgør**, **Accepter**, **Afvis**.
- Sentence case: only the first word capitalized — *Accepter alle ændringer*, *Nyt ord*.
- Decimal comma: *Linjeafstand 1,5*; paper size listed as *A4 (21 × 29,7 cm)*.
- Product and standard names unchanged: **WordCraft**, **Zotero**, **SmartArt**, **WordArt**, **PDF**, **Markdown**, **MCP**, paper sizes (**A4**, **Letter**), citation styles (**APA**, **MLA**, **Chicago**, **Harvard**).
- Placeholders preserved exactly: `{name}`, `{count}`, `{error}`, `{language}`, `{path}` etc.
- Literal `\n` markers preserved exactly as in the source catalog.
- Translated clean-room from plain English meaning in everyday Danish computing vocabulary, not calqued from any other product's Danish UI.
